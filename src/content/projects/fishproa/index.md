  headline: Freshness and quality, at a glance, everywhere.
  text: >
    With fresh food, people judge quality in seconds. The brand had to
    feel clean, coastal and trustworthy, and be simple enough to stay
    consistent wherever it appeared.
  insights:
    - Customers judge freshness and quality at a glance.
