  headline: A new firm that needed to look established from day one.

    BAZ Engineers & Consultants brought together several existing companies

    Austin, Texas. It provides engineering services and refurbished
    equipment for hydrocarbon, petrochemical, industrial and environmental
    projects, working in the US, the Middle East and Latin America. It
    needed a brand that united that experience and felt credible to a
    technical, international audience.

  imageAlt: The BAZ arc symbol over an oil refinery

research:
  headline: Credibility for a technical, international audience.
  text: >
    BAZ's clients are engineering and procurement teams in demanding
    industries. The brand had to be precise and understated, and work
    across regions and languages.
  insights:
    - The audience is technical and values precision over decoration.
    - The brand had to unite the experience of several companies.
    - It needed to work across the US, the Middle East and Latin America.

  headline: A precise, engineering-led mark carried across print, signage, web and presentations.
