problem:
  headline: A learning solution that had to feel unmistakably like the client's brand.
  text: >
    Fujitsu needed a look and feel for its e-learning, including a
    compliance course for people working on the NatWest account and
    training for its Azure sales teams. It had to follow the client's brand
    guidelines closely and speak in the brand's tone, while being engaging
    enough to keep learners moving through the content.
  image: ./module-intro.jpg
  imageAlt: '"Module 1 Introduction" screen with a red title panel over a photo of two colleagues, above a "Fujitsu & NatWest – Our relationship" section with both logos on a map'

research:
  headline: Starting from the client's brand guidelines.
  text: >
    I worked from the client's brand guidelines, covering colour,
    typography, imagery and tone, and translated them into a style that
    works for interactive learning rather than marketing.
  insights:
    - The brand guidelines set the colours, type, imagery and tone.
    - "Learning needs more than branding: clear structure and interactions."
    - The style had to guide every asset produced for the solution.

solution:
  headline: A look and feel built on the brand, with graphics that guide every asset in the solution.
  decisions:
    - title: On brand and in tone
      text: >
        Brand red, clean typography and real-life photography carry the
        brand into every screen, from the course introduction to reflective
        activities about how actions affect NatWest's customers.
      image: ./brand-tone.jpg
      imageAlt: Red "About this course" section with a photo of a man at a laptop, above five red tiles with white icons asking "Consider how your actions could affect NatWest's end customers" over a sky background
    - title: Graphics as a guide for every asset
      text: >
        I created a set of illustrated icons and graphics that show how
        assets across the whole solution should look, so the rest could be
        produced in the same style.
      image: ./icons.jpg
      imageAlt: Course menu cards with red and grey illustrated icons for compliance framework, pre-employment screening, the joiner leaver mover process and information security awareness
    - title: UI and UX shaped by the brand
      text: >
        Progress navigation, clear section headings and video moments keep
        learners oriented through long modules, all within the brand
        guidelines.
      image: ./course-flow.jpg
      imageAlt: Module page with a red progress navigation bar and "The Managed Service Landscape" section, showing a presenter beside a cloud graphic with a play button and the words complexity, agility and dependability
    - title: Interactions that keep learners engaged
      text: >
